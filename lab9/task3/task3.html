London

London is the capital of the United Kingdom. It is known for Big Ben, the Tower of London and Buckingham Palace, as well as its museums, theatres and parks.

Paris

Paris is the capital of France. Its most famous landmark is the Eiffel Tower, and the city is also known for the Louvre museum, fashion, art and food.

Lisbon

Lisbon is the capital of Portugal. It is known for its historical trams, the Belém Tower and viewpoints over the Tagus River, as well as its traditional music and food.

Rome

Rome is the capital of Italy. It is famous for ancient landmarks such as the Colosseum and the Roman Forum, and it also contains the independent state of Vatican City.

Oslo

Oslo is the capital of Norway and lies on the Oslo Fjord. It combines modern architecture with forests, islands and skiing trails.
